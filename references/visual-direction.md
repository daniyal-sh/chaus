## Applied visual direction

Warm emerald, caramel, espresso ink, golden honey, cream paper, lavender and blue flavor accents. Rounded Bricolage lettering and handwritten Kalam accents replace the earlier generic serif direction. Checkerboards, die-cut artwork, crooked paper labels, taped posters, textured fills, ingredient notes, and original social artwork make the brand's visual language part of the layout.

The four new raster illustrations were generated using the built-in image_gen tool with Chaus's form mascot and social artwork as references. They are new website illustrations, not claimed as existing official stickers. Prompts and output paths are recorded in [imagegen-prompts.json](imagegen-prompts.json).

Original social photographs and posters are kept in `public/images/social`; the website links each displayed post to the official source and labels the snapshot September–October 2026. It does not pretend to be a live Instagram integration.
